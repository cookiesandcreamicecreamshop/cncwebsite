Questions

How far ahead should I book?

Two to three weeks for weekends. A week is usually fine on weekdays.

Can we bring decorations or food?

Decorations are welcome, but no confetti or glitter. Pizza and savory snacks are fine; dessert comes from us.

What about allergies?

Tell us in your request. We have gluten-free cookies, but our kitchen handles nuts, gluten and dairy, so we can't promise an allergen-free space.

Is there a deposit?

A deposit holds your date once we confirm. We'll explain the details in our reply.
